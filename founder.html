THE PERSON BEHIND WESTGATE

An international perspective, shaped by experience.

Maxwell Ranson, Founder and CEO of Westgate International Education, comes from a British and Japanese background. Having lived in China and attended an international school, he brings a personal perspective to the opportunities that education can create across cultures.

His own international school experience provides a meaningful point of reference for the questions students and families encounter when exploring different education systems. It is a perspective reflected in Westgate's emphasis on considered guidance and education tailored to individual needs.
